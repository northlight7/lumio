# Ethics, privacy and evaluation

Lumio explores whether practical simulations can help early-career learners understand work and explain their skills. Demonstrating a working product is separate from demonstrating improved employment outcomes.

The intended connection is to quality education, decent work and access to opportunity. No measured learning gain, hiring uplift, fairness result or causal social impact is claimed here. Public company context does not imply partnership, endorsement or a current vacancy. Exercise records and rules are synthetic.

## Risks, controls and limits

| Risk | Implemented controls | Remaining limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Incorrect feedback | Fixed criteria, structured responses, schema checks and code-calculated totals | Valid JSON can still contain wrong or unfair judgments |
| Unequal treatment | Bilingual content, equivalent-answer guidance and visible criteria | Human calibration and subgroup evaluation are still needed |
| Confidential uploads | Ownership declaration, consent, credential screening and bounded parsing | Patterns cannot identify every personal record or trade secret |
| Unwanted disclosure | Company-scoped opt-in, active-share checks, expiry, revocation and minimized responses | Recipients can retain previously downloaded information |
| Outside assistance | In-app help records, disabled assessment coaching and one final assessment submission | External AI, collaboration and published reference answers remain accessible |
| Misleading employment claims | Simulation disclaimers, synthetic demo candidates and criterion explanations | Scores are not validated job-suitability measures |
| Prompt injection | Untrusted-evidence instructions, response validation and adversarial tests | Prompts and schemas cannot eliminate semantic manipulation |

## External AI processing

Grading requests contain the task, reference rubric, submitted decisions, written work and readable extracted attachment content. Attachment filenames, format, parser limitations and hashes are included. Colleague dialogue sends the learner message and attempt context selected by the server.

Original Office binaries are parsed locally rather than uploaded to the provider. Extracted text can still be sensitive. Learners must use synthetic or authorized non-confidential work. AI consent is separate from permission to disclose work to an employer.

A real deployment needs to review provider arrangements, retention rules, notices and applicable obligations. This repository does not establish a provider retention policy or certify legal compliance.

## Learner control

Work starts private. Learners can export data, download original files and erase their account. Original files should be downloaded separately before erasure. Portfolio links select submitted attempts, expire after seven days and can be revoked.

An active link lets its holder view selected submitted evidence and download submitted attachments. Private draft text and chats are excluded. Revocation blocks later server access but cannot recall downloaded copies.

Authenticated employers receive pseudonymous identifiers and sanitized score evidence only while company-specific consent and an active share exist. Private work, originals, identity and chats are excluded. Pseudonymization does not guarantee that people cannot be re-identified in a small group.

Account erasure removes active application records. Backup retention needs separate operational handling. An erase endpoint does not prove immediate deletion from every backup or third-party system.

## Interpreting scores

Scores describe achievement against a particular fictional assignment. They are not qualifications or calibrated predictions of job performance. Complete criterion coverage and transparent ties improve readability but do not prove equal measurement across roles or tasks.

Timing is reported separately from quality. In-app help is recorded, while outside assistance remains unknown. Language, disability, devices, time available and familiarity with workplace tools can affect performance and need study before consequential use.

Reference answers and exemplars are committed for reproducibility. These cases must remain practice or demonstration material. Secure assessments need a separately managed private bank and human governance.

## Evaluation still needed

1. Independent human grading of strong, wrong, paraphrased, concise bilingual and incomplete submissions.
2. Agreement, repeatability, false-pass and false-fail analysis by task, language and relevant participant groups.
3. Learner and accessibility pilots across devices and assistive technologies.
4. Employer review of relevance and understandability, with accountable human decisions and a route to contest errors before consequential use.
5. Separate measurement of learning and employment outcomes through a defined study design.

Automated tests and model-based publication reviews do not complete these evaluation tasks.
